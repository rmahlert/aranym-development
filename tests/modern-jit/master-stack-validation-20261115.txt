Master-stack exception correction, 2026-11-15
===========================================

Reproducer MASTER derives from cpu-boot-baseline:guest-bridge.{c,S}. It uses
real trap/RTE and NatFeat entrypoints, captures the actual frame and returned
A7 before recovering the fixture's caller stack, and needs no disk or network.

Before: current hotpath-patched native Linux binary returned exit8:
  CPU BRIDGE FAIL master SR=3715 frame=00bc PC=01000f52/01000f52
  A7=0101abd0 ISP=0102b04c frameSP=0101abc8 MSPtop=0101abd0
The trap's frame was correct but unconditional clearing of regs.m prevented
RTE from changing from MSP to the requested ISP.

Correction: classify synchronous exceptions and hardware interrupts explicitly.
Synchronous exceptions retain M. Autovector, SCC and MFP interrupts clear M and
use the format0 MSP plus format1 ISP frames when entered from master mode.
The modern dispatch must not inject a hardware interrupt as a synchronous
external exception merely because its PC lies in FastRAM. Dedicated interrupt
entrypoints already decide whether the modern core owns delivery.

MASTER now passes all checks, JIT on and off. New MVBL/MMFP variants use real
VBL and MFP TimerC activity, check actual saved PC/SR, interrupt stack address,
format0 MSP and format1 ISP frames and successful RTE. No host IRQ injection
or memory-witness device is used in these variants. Both pass JIT on and off.
The intermediate sync-only patch failed both hardware cases at frame-SP check
(exit4), exposing the incorrect synchronous modern delivery route.
